> This is the story of a developer, a founder, and a battle for breakthrough.

## A Night of Zero

Today, I stared at my bank balance that read *zero*.  
Rent is due next month. Christmas is in 20 days. My family looks up to me, while I look up to my Creator.

> This isn’t a pity post.  
> This is a battle cry forged in the dark.

Right now, I’m sitting in a dark room, typing on a dying phone. I’m writing this because I need to *remember* this: the silence before the breakthrough.

> One day, I’ll show this to my future children to remind them of the fire their father walked through at 25.

## The Revelation: Tech Is Not Just Code

For years, I believed tech was about tools, syntax, and architecture.

> But the truth hit me harder this year:  
> **Tech is a business.**

Ideas aren’t enough.  
Execution demands power, internet, equipment~and the peace of mind to build without interruption.

Some nights, my greatest battle isn’t the code~it’s the electricity. The data. The battery percentage dropping faster than my hope.

> And yet… I still see the path.

This year alone, I have witnessed new software products being born to life and so many success stories of founders and creators like me

> I know the same God who placed me in this season already sees the exit.

## The Vision: Building RentalWave

> Even in scarcity, the vision remains sharp.

I’m building **RentalWave, a** property marketplace tailored for the African real estate sector. A platform where developers and agents connect directly with buyers and renters. A solution built for the market, I understand, for the continent I love.

My limitation isn’t skill.  
It’s infrastructure: reliable power and unlimited internet.

> A solar setup and a stable wireless connection would completely transform my ability to build.

## A Plea to the Universe~and to You

> Lord, guide me. Direct me.  
> Let Your hands create through me.  
> Turn this darkness into testimony.

And to you reading this right now:

    Your time is a blessing. God bless you for being here.

    Let’s create an impactful marketplace together.
    
* **To Supporters & Future Users**
    
    Follow the journey. Engage with the progress.  
    Your belief is oxygen.
    
* **To Anyone With an Opportunity**
    
    I am a passionate, relentless software engineer.  
    If you have a job, contract, or project—I will deliver with dedication and leave you in awe.
    

> I know this is not the end.  
> This is the valley before the climb.  
> And I will build my way out.

~**Stanley**
